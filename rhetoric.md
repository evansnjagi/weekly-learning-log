### What I covered
Persuasive structure is used when constructing a rhetoric paragraph. The problem comes first explaining to the audience 'the wrong thing' presented. Next, the impact that is/could be caused by the problem at hand is explained, followed by consequences. At the end, a solution is given.

After learning persuasion structure, I started Ethos(appeal to credibility). For ethos to be complete it must show competence, character, and goodwill.

### Key insight
If you miss one of the three items to ethos, your credibility weakens.
