### What I covered
In first phase; I learned about the two math modes used in *Latex*. They are distinct and used for different reasons. 

**Inline math mode** is used when math is embeded inside a sentence. 

**Display math mode** is used when an equation stands on its own.

The second part covered how to write fractions, roots, subscripts and superscripts.

### Key insight
Named operators such as *log* must have a backslash(\\). If the backslash is not there, Latex will treat log as a multiplication of letters: l,o and g, which is mathematically incorrect.
